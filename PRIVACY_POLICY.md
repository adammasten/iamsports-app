# IamSports — Privacy Policy

**Effective date: July 24, 2026**

IamSports ("IamSports," "we," "us") makes an app that helps **adults — parents and coaches — organize, tag, and share youth sports video.** This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. Questions or requests: **adam.admin@iamsports.com**.

## Who the app is for
IamSports is for adults. You must be at least 18 to create an account. The app is **not directed to children, and children do not create accounts.** Adults use it to manage and share sports footage, which may include footage of minors (see "Footage of minors" below).

## Information we collect
- **Account information** — your email address and password (used to sign you in), and the display name and player/profile names you enter.
- **Content you add** — the videos, photos, clips, highlight reels, games, tags, and notes you upload or create in the app.
- **Team and sharing information** — the teams you create or join, and the choices you make about who to share content with (a team wall, a coach, or family).
- **Basic technical information** — information needed to operate the app reliably, such as error and diagnostic logs.

We do **not** run third-party advertising, and we do **not** sell your personal information.

## How we use information
We use your information only to provide and operate the app: to sign you in, store your content, process video (for example, rendering highlight reels), display your content to you, and share it with the specific people and teams **you choose**. We also use it to keep the service secure and to respond to your support requests and reports.

## Who we share information with
- **Service providers who run the app for us**, under agreements to protect your data:
  - **Supabase** — account sign-in, database, and secure video/photo storage.
  - **Railway** — server processing that stitches your clips into highlight reels.
  - **Apple** — app distribution and, if applicable, subscriptions.
- **People you choose to share with** — when you post content to a team wall, send it to a coach, or share it with family, the people you direct it to can see it. You can remove or un-share content in the app.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the safety of people or the public (including reports of child sexual abuse or exploitation — see below).

## Footage of minors
IamSports may contain videos and photos of minors, uploaded by the adults who use it. When you upload such footage, you confirm you have the right to do so and have any required consent from the minor's parent or legal guardian. We keep footage of minors within the sharing boundaries you set — it is **not** made publicly searchable or open to the general internet. We provide in-app tools to **report** content and **block** users, and we act on reports promptly. We strictly prohibit child sexual abuse and exploitation and report apparent violations to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and/or law enforcement as required by law.

## Your choices and rights
- **Access and correction** — you can view and edit your profile and content in the app.
- **Deletion** — you can delete your account at any time from the Account screen. This permanently removes your account and personal data (your login, profile, personal uploads, and reels). Footage you shared with a team may remain with that team so other coaches don't lose it; contact us if you need help.
- **Deactivation** — you can deactivate ("take a break") and reactivate by logging back in, without losing anything.
- **Device permissions** — the app asks permission to access your photo library to upload and to save downloads; you control this in your device settings.

## Data retention and security
We keep your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the app. When you delete your account, we delete your personal data as described above. We use industry-standard measures (including access controls and encrypted transport) to protect your information, though no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

## Children
IamSports is intended for adults. We do not knowingly allow anyone under 18 to create an account or knowingly collect personal information directly from children as users. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will address it.

## Changes to this policy
We may update this policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the effective date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.
