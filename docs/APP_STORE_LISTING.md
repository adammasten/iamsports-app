## Description  (max 4000 chars)

```
IamSports is the all-in-one app for youth-sports coaches and parents to manage game film and team logistics in one place.

Upload full games from your phone, tag the moments that matter, and turn them into shareable highlight reels — then keep the whole team on the same page with schedules, RSVPs, and announcements. Built for the adults who run the team.

FILM & HIGHLIGHTS
• Upload full-game video straight from your phone
• Tag key plays — shots, assists, steals, and more
• Cut clips and build highlight reels in minutes
• Share film and reels to your team or a specific player's wall
• Every video plays clean and full-screen, on phone and web

TEAM SCHEDULE
• One combined schedule across every team you're on
• Games, practices, scrimmages, tournaments, and team events
• One-tap RSVP so coaches know who's coming
• Snack sign-ups, home/away, venue directions, and reminders
• Import a printed or emailed schedule from a photo
• Add it to your phone's calendar or subscribe to the live feed

COACHES' TOOLS
• Roster management and team announcements
• A private coaches' board and playbook
• Per-team roles and permissions

BUILT FOR FAMILIES
• Parents see their kids' film and schedules in one feed
• Share highlights to a child's wall — you stay in control
• Report and block tools keep shared content safe

IamSports is designed for adults — coaches, parents, and guardians — to manage and share youth-sports footage. Children do not create accounts.

Questions or feedback? Reach us at adam.admin@iamsports.com.

Privacy Policy: https://iamsports.com/privacy
Terms of Use: https://iamsports.com/terms
```
